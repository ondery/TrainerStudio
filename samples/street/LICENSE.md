# Sample dataset license

These seven photographs are real street scenes. They replace the earlier synthetic drawings. Each file below is CC0 1.0 on its own Wikimedia Commons page. Commons `LicenseShortName` is CC0, and the license URL is the CC0 1.0 deed. They are not CC-BY, CC-BY-SA, or a non-commercial license.

Faces that were large in the frame, and areas where a vehicle plate could be read, were blurred before the files were saved. The images are about 1600 pixels wide.

The label text in `labels/` was written for this sample. A detector proposed boxes. The boxes were filtered and corrected, then stored as four-point polygons so the folder opens as a segmentation set. A sign and a bicycle use extra vertices. Those label files are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 together with this note.
