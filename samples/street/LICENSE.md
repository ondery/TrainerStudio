# Sample dataset license

The images in this folder are original synthetic drawings made for Trainer Studio
documentation. They are not photographs and they are not taken from any existing
dataset.

The drawings, `data.yaml`, and the label files are dedicated to the public domain
under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). You can copy,
modify, and redistribute them for any purpose.
